# Coffee Talk: Stories Over a Cup of Coffee

*Some stories are easier to tell when there is a warm drink between you and the world.*

## A little introduction

**Coffee Talk** is a narrative-focused game set in an alternate version of Seattle. The player takes the role of a barista working late at night, preparing drinks while listening to the people who visit the café.

Its world is familiar in some ways and fantastical in others. Humans share the city with beings such as elves and orcs, but the conversations often revolve around recognisable things: work, relationships, uncertainty, and the difficulty of understanding one another.

## More than making coffee

The central rhythm of the game is simple: listen to a customer, prepare a drink, and let the conversation unfold. The drink-making is not just a decorative activity. It gives the player a small, hands-on role in each encounter, while the dialogue remains the heart of the experience.

Rather than relying on constant action, the game makes room for pauses. A conversation can drift from an ordinary complaint into something more personal. Different customers bring different worries, and the café becomes a place where their separate lives briefly meet.

## The atmosphere

The late-night setting, gentle music, and pixel-art presentation create an intimate atmosphere. The café feels like a small shelter from the outside world: a place to slow down, listen, and stay for one more conversation.

## A note worth keeping

What makes the premise appealing is its attention to everyday connection. The player is not asked to solve every problem or deliver a perfect answer. Sometimes the meaningful thing is simply to be present, remember what someone said, and offer them a drink.
